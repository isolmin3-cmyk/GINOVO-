Key Benefits of the GINOVO Slope Putting Mat

Perfectly compensates for your putting weaknesses through intuitive and precise data analysis.

01

PATH 31

Provides efficient putting practice through 31 different slope paths by distance and slope.

02

Green speed control

You can adjust the green speed by organizing the texture of the mat with the eraser stick.

03

Concede-Distance Hole-Cup Design

The hole cup and breakaway section were designed to allow practicing putting at acceptable distances.

04

Continuous Practice Without Ball Retrieval

You can practice going back and forth without retrieving the putting ball through various slopes and hole cup areas.
